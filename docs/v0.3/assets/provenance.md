# v0.3 image provenance

Generated with Codex's built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-21. All items below are original game-art candidates. Full generated PNGs are retained in [`generated/`](generated/); runtime WebP files are in `public/assets/v03/` and are indexed in [`manifest.json`](manifest.json). Candidates remain `draft` until reviewed in the integrated, playable game.

The runtime pipeline only crops transparent safety padding, resizes with Lanczos, places the contact point on the V1 anchor, and encodes WebP. It does not add any terrain or combat meaning. The ground material uses a feathered wrap seam; its opposite-edge mean absolute RGB difference is 1.46/255 after final WebP encoding. Transparent unit and prop files retain alpha.

The campaign background is a draft atmosphere asset, not a geographic source of truth. G supplied the canonical 1000×1000 projection and 24 city anchors but no river or mountain control-line API. `campaign-layout-guide-v01.png` places every red anchor from `CITY_GEOGRAPHY` using `projectCampaignPoint`; the turquoise river corridors and charcoal ridges are explicitly illustrative. Exact routes, rivers, ridges, cities and labels remain program-rendered overlays, and the raster should be rejected if integrated overlays do not read clearly. Two distinct imagegen candidates were produced; candidate 02 is selected for its softer water, lower contrast and more open label regions. Both originals are archived below.

The additional `campaign-paper-wash-02` candidate was generated as a neutral substrate after review found that location-specific painted rivers and ridges can diverge from the canonical program geometry. Prompt intent: a square, warm ivory Chinese ink-wash paper surface with low-contrast diffuse edge washes and a broad quiet center; no identifiable river, ridge, road, city, label, symbol, border, text or map landmark. It is a separate review candidate only and is not wired as the active campaign background. Source: `generated/campaign-paper-wash-02-source.png` (1254×1254 RGB); runtime preview: `public/assets/v03/maps/campaign-paper-wash-02.webp` (1000×1000 RGB, Lanczos resize, WebP quality 90). Source SHA-256: `5c5a3e004d96644a52c877ba3cb02a65197fc4ad3cc6a619cd9374366efa9740`; runtime SHA-256: `6dc01b07a5dc024e0208b7f85c4030904b98b6226b75ae69a710c789fb84b94c`. ## Battlefield canvases

Painted 48×32 battlefields are generated by the user in ImageGen from a colour-block reference (`layout.png`) and the full prompt in each folder of [`../battlefield-art/`](../battlefield-art/README.md); every attempt is kept in that folder's `attempts/`. `tools/build-battlefield-canvas.mjs` turns the chosen attempt into the runtime file: Chrome re-encodes it as lossy WebP at the attempt's own 1536×1024 (no resize), and the renderer stretches it over the 3072×2048 world board. Records, including source and runtime SHA-256, are in [`battlefield-canvases.json`](battlefield-canvases.json). The painting carries no rules: terrain comes from the layout, and the art follows it.

- `bf01-north-plain` (① 北方旷野): source `docs/v0.3/battlefield-art/bf01-north-plain/attempts/v4.webp` (prompt: [bf01-north-plain/README.md](../battlefield-art/bf01-north-plain/README.md)), WebP quality 0.9, 618278 bytes, SHA-256 `ff9e0a678961afcaa1d4ef10de7077e343161b07428770bf89116c1a402d558f`.
