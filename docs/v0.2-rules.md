# v0.2 rules reference

Status: v0.2 implements [the gameplay improvement plan](gameplay-improvement-plan.md). This page is the rules reference for everything v0.2 added or changed. The older tables in [implementation.md](implementation.md) still describe the unchanged city actions, economy, and save mechanics. Where the two disagree, this page wins. Constants live at the top of the named source file so a change is a one-line edit.

The ruleset is `baye-atlas-2` and `GameState.version` is 2. Save envelopes use `formatVersion: 2`. Version 1 saves still load: they are validated by the v1 schema, migrated deterministically by `src/game/migrate.ts` (defaults in `MIGRATION_DEFAULTS`, no RNG drawn), then validated again as v2.

Principles that hold throughout:

- Queries, previews, and forecasts never consume RNG.
- Commands are atomic: a rejected command changes nothing.
- No difficulty setting gives anyone free resources or combat bonuses; the AI pays the same costs (design decision D8).
- Every new string is bilingual (English canonical, Chinese catalogs in `src/i18n/*.zh.ts`).

## Tactical battles (`src/game/battle.ts`)

| Rule                | Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Counterattack       | A defender that survives a melee or ranged attack in range counters for `COUNTER_RATE` (50%) of a normal hit, 70% when guarding (`GUARD_COUNTER_RATE`), ×1.1 for fierce officers, minimum 10. Tactics are not countered. The attack forecast shows expected casualties and the counter.                                                                     |
| Tactic points       | Each side starts with `Σ floor(INT/10)` over its officers (cap 30) and gains 2 each day. Costs: fire 4, confuse 3, rally 2, raid supplies 4, ambush 3, rockfall 4; cunning officers pay one less (minimum 1). A tactic with too few points is rejected.                                                                                                     |
| Tactic gating       | Tactics require enough intellect (raid supplies also allows cavalry). Ambush needs a forest tile and a unit that has not moved: it is a ×1.4 attack. Rockfall needs a hill tile, ignores terrain and randomness, and costs the target 10 morale. Raid supplies seizes enemy food (at least 100) and credits half to you. Ambush and rockfall reach 2 tiles. |
| Zone of control     | Entering a tile adjacent to an enemy ends movement. Movement uses a state-expanded Dijkstra; cavalry may slip through one such tile. The reach and stop tiles are drawn on the map.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Morale states       | Below 40 a unit is wavering (×0.85 damage); below 20 it is routed (cannot attack, takes ×1.2). Morale never falls below 5. A unit's death shocks nearby allies by 10 and enemies by 5 morale within 2 tiles; every unit recovers 3 per day. An army whose living units are all routed breaks (reason `rout`).                                               |
| Flanking            | +10% damage for each additional friendly unit adjacent to the target, capped at +30%.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Officer capture     | A unit killed by an adjacent attacker, or by encirclement, may be captured instead of killed. Militia are never captured. Captured officers appear in the aftermath and the prisoner panel.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Supply days         | A march carries food as `days × ceil(troops / 25)` (`SUPPLY_DIVISOR`). The march planner offers 10, 20, or 30 days. Raids carry a fixed 10. The sidebar shows days left for both sides.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Weather             | The next day's weather is decided in advance (`nextWeather`) and shown as a forecast; the forecast draws no RNG beyond what the day change already used.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Day limit           | 30 days (`DEFAULT_DAY_LIMIT`); raids and sacks use `RAID_DAY_LIMIT` (10).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Deployment          | Centre-out (`DEPLOY_ROWS = [5,4,6,3,7,2,8,1,9,0]`) so camp lanes are closed on day one.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Walls and gates     | A city with defense ≥ 60 (`WALL_DEFENSE`) has a wall in column 8 with a gate on rows 4–5. Walls cut received normal damage to ×0.6; gates ×0.75.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Battlefield profile | Each city has a river, mountain, forest, or plains profile that changes the terrain mix. Terrain stays seeded and connected (a BFS check guarantees both camps can reach each other).                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Camp                | Capturing the enemy camp still wins instantly (decision D4). There are no battle deaths (D5).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

### Tactical AI

`battleConfig.tacticalAi` selects `v2` (default) or the preserved `v1`. V2 scores every reachable tile and action: expected damage dealt, counter and threat received (`ATTACK_THREAT_WEIGHT`, `POSITION_THREAT_WEIGHT`), approach pressure (`APPROACH_WEIGHT`), flanking, zone of control, and tactic value against its price. A camp guard is kept only when at least three units remain. A defender who is weaker holds near its camp; a desperate attacker closes in rather than waiting. Auto-resolve uses the same AI with the same rules.

## Campaign (`src/game/campaign.ts` and helpers)

### Plunder, raids, and sacks (`plunder.ts`)

- **Plunder the city** (an action in the _Harsh measures_ tab): your own city; halves farming and commerce, loses public loyalty, takes 8% of the population (floor 500), pays gold and food, and starts a three-month cooldown (`PLUNDER_COOLDOWN_MONTHS`). The preview shows the exact result. Benevolent officers lose extra loyalty. Not available below support 10.
- **Raid** (a march intent): an army with a 10-day raid limit. It plunders on arrival instead of conquering. A raid against an undefended city resolves without a battle.
- **Sack** (a march intent): conquer the city and plunder it in one move.
- Loot is computed by `plunderOutcome` from the city's commerce, farming, and the plunderer's power; the march planner shows the expected loot.

### Persuasion and prisoners

- Persuade chance: `clamp(floor(30 + 0.5·INT − 0.7·loyalty (+20 for a captive)), 5, 75)`; benevolent officers add 8; loyal officers halve it. Rulers cannot be persuaded. Half of a persuaded officer's troops follow.
- Captives can be recruited, **released**, or **executed** from the prisoner panel (`prisoner` command). `Officer.fate` is `active`, `executed`, or `deceased`.
- Officers gain experience levels from battle; levels show on portraits and badges.

### Unrest, departures, diplomacy

- A city below support 30 (`UNREST_SUPPORT`) may revolt; extreme cases become independent.
- In January and July, officers below loyalty 30 (`DEPARTURE_LOYALTY`) may leave; the _loyal_ trait is immune. Officers under 40 are flagged at risk.
- Alliance offers show their success chance; an alliance can be broken at a reputation cost. A faction holding 10 or more cities (`COALITION_CITIES`) can provoke an AI coalition against it.
- **Reorganize troops** (150 gold): change an officer's troop type, losing 20% of their troops (`REORGANIZE_LOSS`).

### Opening truce, seeds, difficulty

- A new campaign can start with a truce of N months (`truceUntilTurn`).
- The seed is random by default; open _Advanced_ on the start screen to type one.
- Difficulty presets (`difficulty.ts`) set only rival aggression, truce length, and whether houses use their personalities: **Story** (cautious, two-month truce, no personalities), **Standard** (balanced, personalities), **Warlord** (aggressive, personalities, no truce).

## Scenarios, endings, optional systems

- **Scenario registry** (`src/content/scenario.ts`): `A divided realm` (190 AD, 24 cities, six houses) and `The eve of Guandu` (200 AD, `src/content/guandu.ts`): Cao Cao, Yuan Shao, Liu Bei, Sun Ce, Liu Zhang, and Ma Teng, with deceased officers recorded as such. Save validation is scenario-aware.
- **Hegemon victory:** hold Luoyang and at least 16 cities for 12 consecutive months (`HEGEMON_MONTHS`). Unifying all cities remains a victory.
- **Aging** (optional, off by default): officers over 60 may die each January; deaths use `fate: 'deceased'` and a `death` report event.
- **Fog of war** (presentation only): rival city details are hidden until scouted; reconnaissance reveals them for two months (`SCOUT_MONTHS`). Rules and AI are unaffected.
- **Officer traits** (`src/content/traits.ts`): content-only tags (for example cunning, fierce, loyal, benevolent) read by the rules through `hasTrait`.
- **Events** (`src/game/events.ts`): two historical once-only events (`anti-dong-coalition`, `dong-burns-luoyang`) fire only when their conditions hold in the current game (D10), plus nine systemic events (plague, bumper harvest, locusts, bandits, wandering officer, come of age, festival, drought, scholar). The coalition event sends the player an invitation they may accept or decline.
- **Adviser and goals** (`src/game/advisor.ts`): pure functions that return up to three suggestions (food, weak border, idle officers, officers at risk), first-campaign goals, and standing objectives. They never change state.
- **AI personalities** (`aiPolicy.ts`, `FACTION_POLICY`): Dong more aggressive with raid bias; Liu does not plunder and favors diplomacy; Sun favors water; Yuan musters more but waits for stronger odds; Yan is bolder; Cao is wary of low supply. Resolved by `policyFor(state, id)`, which respects the `aiPersonalities` setting.

## Presentation

Threat overlay and ZOC stop marks, wall and gate art, morale and level badges, hit flashes and floating numbers (respecting reduced motion), new audio cues, a post-battle recap dialog (`BattleSummary`), _Advance to contact_, a troop legend, the prisoner panel, and the adviser panel. Version labels read v0.2.
