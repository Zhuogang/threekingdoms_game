### Visual corrections verified during implementation

- Fixed floating-point visibility comparisons that could unnecessarily pan a fully fitting board.
- Fixed stale camera focus overriding the currently selected commander.
- Focus the selected city after a new campaign or manual restore/import rather than leaving an old
  distant camera.
- Increased crest/status font sizes in readable mode to 12/14/16 with presets.
- Darkened selected/focus/readiness/attack indicators and faction crest backdrops. Canvas unit text
  and terrain indicators pass the targeted contrast pairs; campaign selection remains distinguishable
  at its pulse minimum.

### Human usability acceptance

The coding-agent functional journey covers at least three months plus a manually commanded
move/rally battle in each language. An independent player has not yet performed the timed
commander-finding or 9-of-10 unit-identification tasks, or evaluated enjoyment. Those human targets
remain **pending** and are not represented as passed by the automated suites.
