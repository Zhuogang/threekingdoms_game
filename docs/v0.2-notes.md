# v0.2 release notes, deviations, and balance evidence

Branch `v0.2`, implementing [gameplay-improvement-plan.md](gameplay-improvement-plan.md). Rules are in [v0.2-rules.md](v0.2-rules.md). Nothing was pushed, merged, or deployed.

## What changed, in one list

- **Battles:** counterattacks, tactic points and three new tactics, zone of control, morale states, flanking, officer capture, supply days, weather forecast, walls and gates, per-city terrain profiles, centre-out deployment, a threat overlay, a post-battle recap, _Advance to contact_, and a second tactical AI (v2) with v1 retained.
- **Campaign:** plunder, raid, and sack; rebalanced persuasion; prisoner release and execution; officer levels; unrest, revolt, and departures; alliance odds, breaking alliances, reputation, and AI coalitions; AI transport, joint attacks, and statecraft; troop reorganization; opening truce; random seeds.
- **Phase 3:** scenario registry with a second scenario (The eve of Guandu), difficulty presets, Hegemon victory, optional aging, fog of war, officer traits, data-driven events, and the adviser and goals.
- **Saves:** `GameState.version` 2 and ruleset `baye-atlas-2`. Version 1 saves migrate deterministically with no RNG drawn.
- **Tooling:** a defensive heuristic player bot (`tools/playerBot.ts`) and `tools/ai-diagnostics.mjs --bot [--scenario=…]` for balance measurement, reporting survival, unified runs, raid outcomes and tactic use per eligible situation; legacy continuation hashes were regenerated deliberately.

## Deviations and judgment calls

These are places where the implementation departs from, or adds to, the plan. Each is deliberate.

1. **Army-break rout rule (added).** An army whose every living unit is routed breaks (battle reason `rout`). Without it, two routed armies could stalemate until the day limit.
2. **AI does not propose alliances to the player.** AI-to-AI diplomacy and coalitions work; offers to the human player are limited to the coalition invitation event.
3. **AI persuasion rarely fires.** The plan's `AI_PERSUADE_CHANCE` (45%) and troop minimum filter out most targets, so the AI persuades far less than it plunders.
4. **`lastBattle` records only battles involving the player** (or none yet), so AI-versus-AI fights never replace the recap the player is looking at.
5. **Invitations exist only for the anti-Dong coalition event.** The general invitation mechanism is in place but only that event uses it.
6. **Second scenario design.** Guandu has deceased officers (kept in the roster with `fate: 'deceased'`), six factions, and uses the same city map with its own ownership and officer placement.
7. **Tactic mix is lopsided.** In diagnostics, confuse and fire make up about three quarters of tactic use; ambush and rockfall are rare because they need specific terrain and an unmoved unit. Measured against eligible situations, the AI ambushes in about a third of the turns it could, but almost never casts rockfall (see the balance table).
8. **Battle-profile visual distinctness is modest.** City profiles change the terrain mix and connectivity, but art and layout remain similar to each other.
9. **Out of scope, as the plan states (§8):** battle randomness changes, duels, camp HP, economy tightening, wounded officers, and battle deaths. D4, D5, and D7 are respected.

## Balance evidence

**Run:** the player bot (`tools/playerBot.ts`) through `tools/ai-diagnostics.mjs --bot` with `DIAGNOSTIC_MONTHS=96` and `--scenario=divided-realm-190,guandu-200`. This is 2 scenarios × 6 houses × seeds 2001–2003 = 36 campaigns of 96 months, and 4,799 battles. Settings: Standard difficulty, Balanced rivals, tactical AI v2.

**Code:** commit `35b51dd` plus uncommitted working-tree changes. These are the review fixes for M1, M2 and M8 in `battle.ts` and `campaign.ts`, and the defensive bot and diagnostics changes for H1. Battles are auto-resolved and every command is replayed without observers to assert that measurement does not change play.

**The bot:** it keeps one officer and at least 40% of a city's strength at home. It never marches from a city that borders a stronger enemy, or when the garrison left behind could be attacked by a neighbouring rival, alone or in a joint attack. It plunders its own city only when the city would otherwise fall, at most once per city per year. It sacks only when the target's support stays at 30 or more. With these rules it survives every 48-month run as an Approachable or Balanced house. The previous bot was eliminated in 25 of 36 probe runs.

How to read the table:

- **Eligible situations for P2-5:** a tactical-AI unit turn that begins with the tactic castable where the unit stands, with a target in range. Rockfall cast after moving onto a hill counts in the total but not as an eligible-in-place cast.
- **Survival** counts a run as eliminated in the month its last city falls. Runs a rival won (by unification or Hegemon) end early and count as survived.
- **Months to unify** is a median over winners only, so it is reported with the number of unified runs.
- **Auto-resolve time:** the campaign figures are within budget. The 10-versus-10 worst case in review M10 is not.

Missed targets, treated as open work:

- **Unification (0 of 36).** The defensive bot holds its ground (median 5 cities at month 96) but does not snowball. This is now a bot limitation as much as a balance result, so it is not evidence that unification is too hard.
- **Battle length and close battles (3 days; 3.8% / 12.9%).** Most battles are lopsided before they start. Contested battles last 6 days but still end in decisive swings. Flanking, counter rates, and the army-break threshold are the likely levers.
- **Raids that fight (99.7%).** Almost every raid target has soldiers, so a raid almost always becomes a battle. Raiders need a way to slip past a thin garrison.
- **Rockfall (0.9% of eligible turns).** The AI seldom casts it, even when it stands on a hill with a target in range.
- **Action mix (move only 61.0%).** This is just over the 60% line.

These numbers come from a heuristic bot rather than human play. They are evidence for the AI and rules, not proof of fun. A human playtest has not been done.
