# v0.2 release notes, deviations, and balance evidence

Branch `hegemony_of_the_three_kindoms_v0.2` (originally `v0.2`), implementing [gameplay-improvement-plan.md](archive/gameplay-improvement-plan.md). Rules are in [v0.2-rules.md](v0.2-rules.md); the overview is [v0.2-summary.md](v0.2-summary.md) and release work is in [next-steps.md](next-steps.md). The October 20 implementation and current verification are recorded in [v0.2-validation.md](v0.2-validation.md).

## What changed, in one list

- **Battles:** counterattacks, tactic points and three new tactics, zone of control, morale states, flanking, officer capture, supply days, weather forecast, walls and gates, per-city terrain profiles, centre-out deployment, a threat overlay, a post-battle recap, _Advance to contact_, and a second tactical AI (v2) with v1 retained.
- **Campaign:** plunder, raid, and sack; rebalanced persuasion; prisoner release and execution; officer levels; unrest, revolt, and departures; alliance odds, breaking alliances, reputation, and AI coalitions; AI transport, joint attacks, and statecraft; troop reorganization; opening truce; random seeds.
- **Phase 3:** scenario registry with a second scenario (The eve of Guandu), difficulty presets, Hegemon victory, optional aging, fog of war, officer traits, data-driven events, and the adviser and goals.
- **Saves:** `GameState.version` 2 and ruleset `baye-atlas-2`. Version 1 saves migrate deterministically with no RNG drawn.
- **Tooling:** reproducible campaign/battle diagnostics with raw counts, explicit victory types, censored checkpoints and source/bundle fingerprints. The frontier player bot transports resources and rehearses joint attacks; the historical baseline and turtle policies remain available. Legacy continuation hashes are deliberately regenerated after rule changes.

## Deviations and judgment calls

These are places where the implementation departs from, or adds to, the plan. Each is deliberate.

1. **Army-break rout rule (added).** An army whose every living unit is routed breaks (battle reason `rout`). Without it, two routed armies could stalemate until the day limit.
2. **AI does not propose alliances to the player.** AI-to-AI diplomacy and coalitions work; offers to the human player are limited to the coalition invitation event.
3. **AI persuasion rarely fires.** The plan's `AI_PERSUADE_CHANCE` (45%) and troop minimum filter out most targets, so the AI persuades far less than it plunders.
4. **`lastBattle` records only battles involving the player**, so AI-versus-AI fights never appear in the recap the player is looking at.
5. **Invitations exist only for the anti-Dong coalition event.** The general invitation mechanism is in place but only that event uses it.
6. **Second scenario design.** Guandu has deceased officers (kept in the roster with `fate: 'deceased'`), six factions, and uses the same city map with its own ownership and officer placement.
7. **Tactic mix depends on eligibility.** In the historical diagnostics below, confuse and fire dominate total casts; ambush and rockfall require particular terrain and an unmoved unit. Eligible-in-place use was 25.9% for ambush and 9.0% for rockfall, both above their 5% targets.
8. **Battle profiles share a visual system.** City profiles change terrain mix and connectivity. The October 20 presentation pass adds distinct plains, river, mountain and forest palettes and bilingual profile labels; it retains the parchment/ink style.
9. **Out of scope, as the plan states (§8):** battle randomness changes, duels, camp HP, economy tightening, wounded officers, and battle deaths. D4, D5, and D7 are respected.

## Final October 20 balance evidence

The final defaults are camp radius 4 and normal/fire/rockfall rates 0.18/0.1584/0.1296: a proportional 28% damage reduction. Counters remain 0.5/0.7; campaign AI attack margins, instant camp capture and battle randomness rules are unchanged. Thirteen controlled configurations were compared with the historical bot frozen. The retained baseline policy also matched the pinned `077556c` bot across 432 monthly command sequences and states. The improved frontier bot uses legal convoys, rehearsed two-city attacks and strategic target ranking.

Final runs use Standard difficulty, Balanced rivals, tactical AI v2 and both scenarios, with no tuning overrides. Development seeds 2001–2003 and held-out seeds 2101–2103 each cover 36 campaigns through 96 months or their actual terminal outcome. These produced 5,168 and 5,980 observed battles. A separate 48-month frontier run supports comparison at the original horizon. Every diagnostic command is replayed without observers and checked for equal resulting state. The actual-default development replay exactly matches all 36 earlier numeric-override campaigns, including outcomes, checkpoints and state hashes.

The [durable evidence JSON](assets/v0.2-balance-evidence.json) retains configurations, raw counts, source/bundle hashes, outcomes, checkpoints, retention and equivalence proofs. Its candidate manifest identifies the tested runtime/assets/tests/tools by content. The metadata revision `077556c` is the base commit before these implementation changes were committed, not a claim that the unmodified base produced the results.

### Guandu turtle gate and Yuan Shao

The final 12-month turtle run, seeds 2001–2003 on actual defaults, passes D10's six-month gate: Ma Teng 3/3, Liu Bei 3/3 and Liu Zhang 2/3 keep every starting city through month 6. Liu Zhang's first losses are month 11, month 5 and none. Their twelve-month retention is 3/3, 3/3 and 1/3 respectively. Large-house border losses remain context under D10.

Yuan Shao stays Approachable. All six normal-bot Guandu runs retain surviving cities through 96 months or an earlier rival Hegemon ending, despite losing the first city in month 2. Three end through rival Hegemon victories and none wins. The rating reflects opening resilience; ease of human play remains unobserved. Reclassifying him would also introduce Standard's two-month truce and alter the measured opening.

Level pips are shared by React and Pixi: level 0 has none, 1–2 one, 3–4 two and 5 three. Four battlefield palettes and a bilingual sidebar profile row are integrated. The user approved the final four profile screenshots and Ma Chao's portrait/contact sheet on October 20.

All previous 106 renditions and 53 provenance entries were preserved. Empty or absent source-directory reruns preserve the complete output unchanged.

Rendering now stops when idle and reuses cached artwork/layers, with explicit invalidation after state, input, locale, overlay, resize and animation changes. Tests measure actual Pixi draws separately from browser animation callbacks. The DPR cap remains 2 after 2-versus-1.5 comparisons; multisample antialiasing is disabled. The final FPS matrix and integrated checks are recorded in [the validation record](v0.2-validation.md). Software rendering remains a measured limitation. Four human gameplay sessions and a physical low-end-machine check were explicitly deferred by D11; no such observations are claimed.

## Historical balance evidence

**Run:** the player bot (`tools/playerBot.ts`) through `tools/ai-diagnostics.mjs --bot` with `DIAGNOSTIC_MONTHS=96` and `--scenario=divided-realm-190,guandu-200`. This is 2 scenarios × 6 houses × seeds 2001–2003 = 36 campaigns of 96 months, and 5,941 battles. Settings: Standard difficulty (including the opening truce for Challenging houses), Balanced rivals, tactical AI v2. Battles are auto-resolved and every command is replayed without observers to assert that measurement does not change play.

**Code:** commit `a1ddf12` (tracks T1–T5 of [the v0.2 review](archive/v0.2-review.md)). These figures describe that historical rules/bot combination, also used through `8e93b10`; they are not final measurements of the October 20 implementation. The new measurement tools distinguish player/rival Hegemon wins, true unification, elimination and unfinished runs, and record horizon censoring explicitly.

**The bot:** it keeps one officer and at least 40% of a city's strength at home, never marches from a city that borders a stronger enemy, plunders its own city only when it would otherwise fall, and sacks only when support stays at 30 or more. From month 24, or when a city is more than twice as strong as its strongest neighbour, it keeps only 25% at home.

How to read the table:

- **Eligible situations for P2-5:** a tactical-AI unit turn that begins with the tactic castable where the unit stands, with a target in range. Rockfall cast after moving onto a hill counts in the total but not as an eligible-in-place cast.
- **Survival** counts a run as eliminated in the month its last city falls. Runs a rival won (by unification or Hegemon) end early and count as survived.
- **Months to unify** is a median over winners only, so it is reported with the number of unified runs.

At that historical revision, the following misses remained open. The final implementation and explicit D8/D9 dispositions are recorded above:

- **Unification (0 of 36).** The bot's offensive mode helps (it now averages 4.2 cities at month 96 and peaks at 13), but it does not snowball to 24 cities. Rival houses reach 21 cities in these runs, and in 3 of the 36 runs one was proclaimed Hegemon. Unification still cannot be measured with this bot; it is a bot limit more than evidence about the rules.
- **Battle length and close battles (4 days; 3.8% / 10.9%).** 2,440 of 3,729 sampled battles end by elimination in a median of 3 days, because the AI attacks only when it is far stronger. Contested battles last 6 days but still end in decisive swings. Flanking, counter rates, the army-break threshold, and the AI attack thresholds are the levers; none was tuned in this pass.
- **Action mix (move only 61.2%).** Just over the 60% line, and unchanged by the rule changes.

These numbers come from a heuristic bot rather than human play. They are evidence for the AI and rules, not proof of fun. A human playtest has not been done.
